Liquidity Ratios

Current Ratio

Measures a company’s ability to pay short-term obligations using current assets. A healthy range is 1.5–3.0, indicating strong liquidity without excess idle assets.

Acid-Test

A stricter liquidity measure that shows whether a company can meet short-term liabilities using only its most liquid assets (excluding inventory). A good range is 1.0–2.0.

Solvency Ratios

Debt-to-Equity Ratio

Indicates how much a company is financed by debt compared to shareholders’ equity. A range of 0.5–1.5 suggests balanced financial leverage, while higher values imply increased risk.

Debt-to-Asset Ratio

Shows the proportion of a company’s assets financed through debt. A healthy range is 0.3–0.6 (30%–60%), reflecting manageable reliance on borrowed funds.
